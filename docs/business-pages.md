## Evidence ledger

Original references are retained internally in `docs/research-sources.md`; public attribution remains beside the claims. Selection notes:

| Source / customer | Meaning and limits |
| --- | --- |
| ServiceTitan / Bill Joplin's | Estimated incremental booked revenue. Do not add operating-cost savings into profit or assign the separate call-count window to revenue. |
| Birdeye / 000 Plumbing Services Australia | Call growth with several tools. Generic growth figures elsewhere on the provider page are not this customer's results. |
| Birdeye / H2Pro Plumbing | Jobs reported in a webchat case with other communications tools. No controlled attribution. |
| Tradify / Kennett Electrics | Administration time across multiple tasks. Neither quoting-only nor an AI effect. |
| Retell / ISpeedToLead | Average response time in a real-estate lead marketplace. Prefer the clear time measure over the ambiguous booking-rate uplift. |
| Tradify / Fitted Joinery Installation | Prepared quote kits and delegation. Keep the manual approval context. |
| Google web.dev / Vodafone | Controlled performance test, relative lead-rate improvement. Not chatbot evidence or a small-business forecast. |
| CallRail consumer survey | Reported past behavior among respondents, not a fraction of missed calls lost. |
| BrightLocal consumer survey | Self-reported preferences, not measured revenue or causal effects. |
| Google ranking guidance | Relevance, distance, and prominence; no fixed ranking promise. |

Research also screened other provider cases, academic papers, and original reports. Excluded prominent claims:

- The circulated $126,360 missed-call-loss number is illustrative arithmetic, not a measured small-business average.
- ServiceTitan's Brothers case has inconsistent adoption/results dates; don't use its large revenue total without clarification.
- Deloitte's 21.6% statistic describes progression between form steps; overall lead-generation conversion in that report declined 1.9%. Do not label it 21.6% more leads.
- BrightLocal's Anthem archive mixes an occupancy level with percentage-growth wording. Use the original narrative if this case is considered later.
- Smith.ai's Native PT booking figure applies only to a selected group of message-leaving leads, without a sample or time window.
