Receptionist-only scope

- Approved business answers and inquiry capture.

- Structured staff handoff and an owned callback.

- Human fallback for uncertainty or a failed handoff.

- Basic monitoring and a simple report.

- Included usage stated and costed in the quote.

Direct booking, custom CRM connections, live inventory, website/search work, additional departments or campaigns, and unlimited changes are outside this base scope.

Combined scope

Define website pages, content owner, revision rounds, hosting/domain arrangements and maintenance. Define SEO/AEO priorities, reporting and access. Define receptionist usage and routing independently.

Combining services does not make custom CRM, live inventory, direct booking, paid ads, financing workflows or unlimited revisions included. Extra work requires explicit scope and verified access.

No guaranteed rankings, AI mentions, sales or revenue.
