Project inquiries
When you submit an inquiry, Schurr Automations stores the name, business name, contact details, project interest, message, preferred contact method, and contact permission you provide. Only the contact detail for your chosen response method is required. This information is used to review your request, contact you through that method, and discuss a potential engagement. When owner email alerts are enabled, Resend processes the contact details and project message you submit to notify Gavin by email. The notification links to the protected workspace. Its sending status is recorded separately from your saved inquiry: an email failure does not remove your request, and provider acceptance is not proof that an alert was delivered or read. Gavin may keep related business contact details, notes about conversations, sales status, and follow-up tasks in a private customer relationship management workspace. A fit-call request made from an approved dealer or QR campaign includes its non-sensitive source code in a separate CRM field. It describes the source link, not a verified identity or cross-device journey. If you request the optional automatic email, Resend processes your email address and the message content to send one inquiry confirmation with information about our services and a booking link. We save your choice and the sending status. Replies go to Gavin’s business inbox at gavin@schurrautomations.com. Submitting a form does not subscribe you to a mailing list.
Requested AI callbacks and appointments
If you separately request an AI callback on the website, we record that permission and send your phone number to Retell to make one automated call about your inquiry. The assistant identifies itself as AI, can explain our services, and can help you choose an available appointment. This optional permission is not required to purchase services. You can decline further contact during the call or contact Gavin directly. We do not enroll past inquiries in AI callbacks. Unconfirmed call attempts are held for review rather than automatically redialed. The public fit-call form records your preferred time, timezone, and contact details as a request for Gavin to review. It does not confirm or reserve an appointment. Gavin confirms the time separately. For existing calendar appointments, we store the time, timezone and contact details, and use Resend to notify Gavin about bookings, reschedules and cancellations. External calendars are not automatically synced. Request changes or cancellations by contacting Gavin.
Business outreach
Schurr Automations may keep publicly available business contact information, source websites, research notes, outreach drafts, and conversation history in the private workspace to assess relevant business services. Publicly listed contact details are reviewed before use. Requests for no further outreach are kept in a suppression list. Public website research may be processed by a research provider when that service is connected.
Business email connection
When Gavin authorizes the Gmail connection, the private workspace stores only sent messages and replies linked to CRM conversations for follow-up tracking. Gmail connection credentials are encrypted. Unrelated mailbox messages are not imported.
Access and service providers
Inquiry records are available through Gavin’s protected owner account. Website hosting and database providers process the records to operate this website. Please do not include passwords, confidential customer records, payment information, or other sensitive information in the form.
Website measurement
The website counts page views and selected actions, including phone-link clicks, fit-call button clicks, demonstration actions, brief views, and PDF downloads.
