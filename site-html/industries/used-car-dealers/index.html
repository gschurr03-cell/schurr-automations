Before we connect anything.

Can you use our CRM?

We inspect the subscription, permissions and supported read/write actions. A staff task inbox may be the simpler first option. We do not promise a connection before testing it.

Can it tell someone whether a vehicle is available?

Only when the contracted setup has a suitable, current source and the correct uncertainty rules. Otherwise it requests staff confirmation.

Can it book appointments?

A supported booking path must check availability and confirm that the appointment was saved. Without that proof, it captures a request for staff.

What do you measure?

Eligible inquiries captured, assigned callbacks, callback completion, requests versus confirmed appointments, unresolved cases, usage and failures. Sales attribution requires your records and careful interpretation.
