The practical details

Before we build.

What does AEO mean?

Answer engine optimization makes business information and useful answers easier for search and answer tools to understand. It complements sound SEO and does not guarantee inclusion. We do not sell a special AI file or schema as a universal ranking switch.

How do review requests work?

Invite eligible customers to share an honest experience. We do not filter requests by expected rating, buy reviews, or offer an incentive for a Google review. Staff can review draft complaint responses without suppressing legitimate feedback.

Do you manage Google Business Profile?

Eligible profiles and accurate business information can be included. The scope depends on your real locations, permissions, and existing setup.

When will we see results?

Timing depends on the starting point, market, and competition. Reporting distinguishes completed work from observed changes. We do not promise a ranking, AI mention, traffic level, or revenue deadline.
