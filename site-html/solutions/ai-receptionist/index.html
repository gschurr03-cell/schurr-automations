The practical details

Before we build.

Can it use our booking system?

Direct booking is a separately scoped addition. It is enabled only when the connection supports availability checks, successful write confirmation, cancellation, and duplicate protection. The receptionist-only scope captures an appointment request for staff to confirm.

What if nobody answers a transfer?

Before launch, we agree the fallback: for example, capture a callback request and notify the responsible staff member. The caller is told what happened; an unanswered transfer is never described as successful.

What data do you need?

We start with approved dealership information, business rules, staff destinations, and the minimum contact details for a useful handoff. These conversations must not collect credit applications, financial or identity documents, or passwords.

What happens during an outage?

We define the available routing fallback and recovery steps for the selected providers. We test failure cases, identify who receives alerts, and agree support hours before launch.

What does the recurring service include?

Setup is a separate fee, quoted for the agreed scope. Your recurring quote states management, included usage, reporting, and support. Direct booking, custom CRM connections, live inventory, additional departments, campaigns, and unlimited changes are not included in the receptionist-only scope. Website, SEO, and AEO work each have a separate scope, including when combined in one proposal.

What will it not do?

It will not invent availability, approve financing, promise a final quote, or claim a transfer or booking succeeded without confirmation. Decisions outside approved rules go to your team.
