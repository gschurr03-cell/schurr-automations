The practical details

Before we build.

What does the proposal specify?

Page count, content responsibilities, revision rounds, hosting, domain ownership, integrations, maintenance, and exclusions. You can see what is included before work begins.

Can you rebuild our existing website?

Yes. We review useful content, existing URLs, the domain, and current connections first. Where a URL must change, redirects and linked materials are planned together.

Can it connect to booking and follow-up?

Where the selected tools support it. We scope the connection and test its success and failure paths. An appointment request is labelled as a request until the booking is confirmed. Inventory feeds and live availability are included only when separately scoped and verified.

Is ongoing SEO included?

Technical search foundations are part of the agreed website build. Ongoing SEO, AEO, reviews, advertising, and new automation have their own scope unless your proposal explicitly includes them.
