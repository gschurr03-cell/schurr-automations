Who owns your next vehicle inquiry?

Review a small, permissioned sample of recent vehicle inquiries. Use a reference or initials instead of buyer names and contact details. Keep credit documents, identity information and other sensitive records out of this worksheet. No email address or marketing signup is required.

Five questions to review

  1. Did the buyer’s inquiry reach the intended place?
  2. Were enough details captured?
  3. Did one salesperson own the callback?
  4. Was the agreed next action completed?
  5. Can the team verify the final outcome?

This worksheet identifies questions to investigate. It is not a revenue-loss estimate or a diagnosis of your dealership. A useful next step is to review one recurring gap together.
