Three practical questions

A result your team can verify.

  1. 01

    What repeatedly gets missed or delayed?

    Look at a permissioned sample of recent vehicle inquiries and callback records. Separate unique buyers from repeat calls. Identify the missing next action and which staff member owns it.

  2. 02

    What is a completed outcome worth?

    Track callbacks completed, visits requested, visits confirmed, visits attended and vehicles purchased as separate outcomes. Compare vehicle contribution after direct costs with the cost of the workflow; the sale price is not profit. Staff time returned may create capacity without reducing payroll.

  3. 03

    What will change, and how will we know?

    Agree the baseline period, data source, outcome, and review date. Include implementation, monthly service, usage, and any new software costs when judging value.

The comparison must include what happened, over which period, and what remains uncertain. A task created, an alert sent, or a demo completed is not proof of a sale.

What counts as evidence

Demonstration first. Your baseline next.

A labelled dealer demonstration can show a vehicle inquiry, a salesperson handoff, and a failure path. It does not prove a result for your dealership.

External research can inform a question, but its population and measured outcome matter. It is not a forecast of your revenue or a Schurr client result. We use your own records to decide whether the proposed scope makes sense.
