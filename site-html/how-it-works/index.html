Four clear stages

From the first question to a working handoff.

  1. 01

    A 15-minute fit call.

    Bring one recent vehicle inquiry, the tools you use, and the staff role responsible for follow-up. You leave with a useful starting point, or a simpler option if your current setup can solve it.

  2. 02

    Workflow and scope.

    For a suitable project, we arrange a separate 35-minute discovery. You supply business rules, access requirements, and an operational owner. You receive a written scope, costs, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria. Setup is a separate fee, quoted for that scope. Each service has its own deliverables and ownership, including when combined in one proposal.

  3. 03

    Build and acceptance.

    You provide agreed access, approved dealership information, and timely decisions. We test normal inquiries and failure cases together, including unanswered transfers, unavailable vehicle information and unsaved callback tasks where relevant to the scope. We show what passes before launch.

  4. 04

    Launch and management.

    Your team receives handoff training and clear ownership. Ongoing management, usage, reporting, and correction arrangements are set out in the agreement. Support on extended weekdays, 7am to 7pm MT. Gavin provides solo coverage. Automated availability is separate from staffed support hours.

The implementation timeline starts when the scope, access, business rules, and approvals are ready. We agree the schedule for your project; there is no universal launch deadline.
